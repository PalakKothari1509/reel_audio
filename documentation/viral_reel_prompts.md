# Ready-to-Use Viral Reel Prompts

> **In-App Source**: These prompts are also embedded directly in `lib/content_ideas.dart` as the `readyPrompt` field on each `HookIdea`. When a hook has a pre-built viral prompt, the offline generator will use it automatically — no manual copy-paste needed.

High-converting, viral Reels blueprint for the top 10 strongest story hooks from your library.

## Production Standards
- **Duration:** 20 seconds (optimal for completion rate and replay)
- **Format:** 2 parts of 10 seconds each (20-second combined)
- **Visual:** 3D Pixar feature-film style
- **Characters:** Ria (pigtails, pink star dress), Mumma (yellow kurti, jeans)
- **Text:** High-contrast bold text overlays are added by the editor after generation — the video model must NOT render text. Each part specifies an "On-Screen Text" section for post-generation overlay.

## Quick Tips for Production

1. **Pacing:** Each 10-second prompt has 2 distinct visual beats to ensure high energy.
2. **Text Placement:** Keep the text hooks vertically centered or upper-third so Instagram's interface icons (heart, comments, audio title) don't cover them.
3. **Consistency:** Whenever you feed these into the video model, attach the reference images of Ria and Mumma just like before to keep faces, skin tones, and outfits consistent.
4. **No moral on screen:** The lesson is implied by the story, not stated as text.
5. **Text cap:** Maximum 5 words per overlay segment for readability.
