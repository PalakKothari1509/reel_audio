📈 HOW TO DECIDE WHAT TO MAKE MORE OF

This is very important.

Don't decide based only on likes.

For each Reel, record:

Metric	What we're looking for
Non-follower reach	Discovery
Average watch time	Story quality
Shares	Relatability
Saves	Value
Comments	Community
Profile visits	Curiosity
Follows	Actual conversion

Instagram itself has provided Reels insights that break down follower/non-follower reach and retention-related metrics, so use those rather than judging a Reel only from total views.

Example:

Suppose:

Reel A
10K views
50 follows

Reel B
5K views
150 follows

For your goal, B tells us something very valuable: fewer people watched, but more viewers decided the account was worth following.

So we reproduce the idea/style of B, not automatically A.
